================================================================
  CONCLUSION
================================================================

  For yield prediction (regression), Gradient Boosting on the
  original merged dataset gives the best result with R²=0.9455.
  This means the model can explain 94.5% of the variation in
  crop production across different districts and seasons.

  For crop recommendation (classification), DNN on the merged
  dataset achieves the highest accuracy of 94.03%, correctly
  identifying the best crop to grow in 94 out of 100 cases.

  Data augmentation (50k rows) improves consistency across
  models but does not surpass the original merged dataset for
  either task. This suggests the original data is well
  representative of real agricultural patterns in Bangladesh.
